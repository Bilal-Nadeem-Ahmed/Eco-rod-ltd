Drains in Nottingham Homes

A lot of Nottingham's housing is Victorian and Edwardian terraces, in areas like Sneinton, Radford, Lenton and Hyson Green. Many of these streets have old clay drains, and neighbouring houses often share a drain run before it reaches the public sewer.

That means one blockage can affect several houses, and it isn't always obvious whose responsibility it is. We find where the blockage is and tell you straight away whether it's yours to fix or Severn Trent's.

The problems we see most often in Nottingham:

  • Shared drains blocking under terraced streets
  • Tree roots in older clay pipes
  • Grease and wipes in rented and student houses
  • Overflowing gullies after heavy rain

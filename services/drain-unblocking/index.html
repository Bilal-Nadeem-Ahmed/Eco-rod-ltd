Signs You Have a Blocked Drain

Most blocked drains give you a warning before they overflow. Call us if you notice any of these:

  • Sinks, baths or showers draining slowly
  • Gurgling noises from plugholes or the toilet
  • Bad smells inside the property or around outside drains
  • Water rising in the toilet bowl when you flush
  • An outside gully or manhole overflowing

If several sinks, toilets or showers are affected at the same time, the blockage is usually further down the system, in the main drain or an inspection chamber, rather than in a single pipe.

What Causes Blocked Drains?

  • Fat, oil and grease Poured down the kitchen sink as a liquid, it cools, sets hard and catches everything that follows.
  • Wet wipes and sanitary products Even wipes labelled "flushable" don't break down like toilet paper and are one of the most common causes of blocked toilets and drains.
  • Leaves, silt and debris Outside gullies collect garden waste and soil, especially after heavy rain.
  • Tree roots Roots work their way into cracked or loose pipe joints and grow into a mat that traps waste.
  • Damaged or collapsed pipes If a drain keeps blocking, the pipe itself may be cracked, sagging or collapsed. A CCTV drain survey will show exactly what's wrong, and we can carry out any drain repairs needed.

See Inside Your Drains Without Digging

We send a waterproof camera through your drains to find blockages, cracks, root ingress and collapsed sections that you can't see from the surface.

You get a clear answer about what is wrong and what needs doing, before anyone starts digging.

When Do You Need a CCTV Drain Survey?

A survey is worth it if:

  • A drain keeps blocking in the same place
  • There are bad smells you can't trace
  • You see damp, subsidence or rats near a drain
  • You're planning an extension near a drain
  • You want to know the condition of the drains in a property

A survey costs far less than digging in the wrong place, and it tells you whether a repair is actually needed.

What a CCTV Survey Can Find

  • Cracks and fractures Ground movement and age crack pipes, letting water out and roots or rats in.
  • Root ingress Tree roots find their way into small gaps at the pipe joints and grow until they block the pipe.
  • Collapsed sections Old clay pipes can break and partly collapse, causing repeated blockages.
  • Displaced joints Joints that have moved out of line create a lip where waste catches.
  • Build up and blockages Grease, scale and debris show up clearly on camera, so we know whether drain cleaning will fix the problem or drain repairs are needed.

How Our CCTV Drain Surveys Work

  1. Book your survey Call us and we'll arrange a time that suits you. For urgent problems we're available 24/7.
  2. We prepare the drain If the drain is blocked or dirty, we clear it first so the camera gets a clear view.
  3. We survey the drain We feed the camera along the pipe and record its condition as it goes.
  4. We explain what we found We talk you through what the camera shows while we're on site.
  5. You get a written report We send you a written report of the findings with our recommendations for any repair or maintenance.

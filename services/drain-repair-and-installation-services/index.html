A damaged drain doesn't fix itself. Left alone, it keeps blocking, leaks into the ground and can let rats into your property.

We find exactly where the damage is, explain what needs doing and agree a fixed price before any work starts.

Signs Your Drain Needs Repairing

Call us if you notice any of these:

  • A drain that keeps blocking after it's cleared
  • Damp patches near an outside drain
  • Sinking paving or ground above a drain
  • Bad smells outside with no obvious cause
  • Rats in or around the property

Many of these can have other causes, so we always confirm the problem before recommending a repair.

What Damages Drains?

  • Tree roots Roots push into pipe joints and cracks, then grow until they split the pipe or block it.
  • Ground movement Settlement, heavy vehicles and building work can crack pipes or pull joints apart.
  • Age Older clay pipes and pitch fibre pipes become brittle and can collapse.
  • Repeated blockages Constant pressure from blockages, and the wrong tools used to clear them, can damage the pipe.
  • Poor original work Badly laid pipes with dips or poor joints cause problems for years. A CCTV drain survey shows exactly what is there.

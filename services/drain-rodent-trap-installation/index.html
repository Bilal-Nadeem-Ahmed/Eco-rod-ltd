Stop Rats Getting In Through Your Drains

Rats live in the sewers and can travel up drain pipes into homes and businesses. Poison and traps inside the property don't stop more arriving.

A drain rodent trap, often called a rat blocker, is fitted inside your drain. Waste flows out as normal, but rats can't get back in.

Signs Rats Are Using Your Drains

Call us if you notice any of these:

  • Scratching noises in walls, floors or ceilings
  • Droppings near drains, sinks or toilets
  • Gnaw marks on pipes or wood
  • Rats seen near outside drains or manholes
  • A pest controller has said rats are coming from the drains

Rats getting in from the drains will keep coming back until the route is blocked, however many are caught inside.

How Rats Get In Through Drains

  • Through the sewer connection Rats swim and climb up the pipe from the public sewer into your private drain.
  • Cracked or broken pipes Damaged pipes give rats a way out of the drain into the ground under your property. We can carry out any drain repairs needed.
  • Old or disused pipes Unused connections from old toilets, sinks or extensions can be left open under floors.
  • Missing or broken covers Damaged inspection chamber covers let rats in and out of the drain.
  • Unknown defects If it's not clear how rats are getting in, a CCTV drain survey can find the route.

How We Fit a Drain Rodent Trap

  1. Get in touch Tell us what's happening. For urgent problems we're available 24/7.
  2. We inspect the drain We open the inspection chambers and look for signs of rats and any damage to the drain.
  3. We confirm the price We agree a fixed price with you before any work starts.

Fast Toilet Unblocking for Homes and Businesses

A blocked toilet is never something you want to wait on, especially if it is the only one in the property.

We clear the blockage, check the pipework beyond the toilet is flowing, and leave the bathroom clean.

Signs Your Toilet Is Blocked

Call us if you notice any of these:

  • Water rising in the bowl when you flush
  • The toilet flushing slowly or not at all
  • Gurgling from the toilet or nearby plugholes
  • Bad smells from the toilet or bathroom
  • Water backing up into the bath or shower

If the bath, shower or downstairs sinks are also slow, the problem is probably in the drain outside rather than the toilet itself. We check both.

What Causes Blocked Toilets?

  • Wet wipes Even wipes labelled "flushable" don't break down like toilet paper. They are the most common cause of blocked toilets we see.
  • Sanitary products and nappies These swell with water and lodge in the bend of the toilet or further down the pipe.
  • Too much toilet paper A large amount flushed at once can block a toilet with a low flush or a long pipe run.
  • Objects dropped in Toys, toothbrushes and air fresheners often end up in the U-bend, especially in homes with young children.
  • A blocked drain further down If the main drain is blocked, the toilet is often the first place you notice it. We may need to clear the drain itself with drain unblocking.

How We Unblock Your Toilet

  1. Call us, day or night We're available 24/7, including evenings and weekends. Tell us what's happening and we'll get an engineer on the way.
